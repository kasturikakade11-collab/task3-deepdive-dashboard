# Deep-Dive Analysis Report: RFM Customer Segmentation

# Methodology
RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) segments customers 
based on how recently, how often, and how much they buy.

# Segmentation Rules
| Segment | Recency | Frequency | Monetary |
|---------|---------|-----------|----------|
| Champion | ≤90 days | ≥2 orders | ≥₹2,00,000 |
| Loyal Customer | ≤180 days | Any | ≥₹1,50,000 |
| Recent Low Spender | ≤90 days | Any | <₹1,00,000 |
| At-Risk High Value | >180 days | Any | ≥₹1,50,000 |
| Regular Customer | All others | - | - |



# Key Findings
## Finding 1: Champion Segment
Champions make up 1.94% of total customers but generate 
5.03% of total revenue, confirming that a small high-value 
group drives disproportionate business impact.

## Finding 2: At-Risk High Value
173 customers fall into At-Risk High Value, 
representing ₹46,947,600.93 in revenue that could be lost without
intervention—making this the top priority segment for retention campaigns.
## Finding 3: Electronics Dominates
Electronics accounts for the top category across 5 out of 5 customer segments, 
showing demand for this category isn't limited to one type of customer—it's broadly popular.

# Business Recommendations
## Champion Customers
- Exclusive early access to new products
- Loyalty rewards program
- Personal account manager

## At-Risk High Value Customers
- Immediate win-back campaign
- Personalized discount offers
- Direct outreach from sales team

## Recent Low Spenders
- Upselling campaigns
- Bundle offers
- Category recommendations based on 
  Electronics dominance finding
