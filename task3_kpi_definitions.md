# Core KPI Definitions — Task 3

> KPI 1: Total Revenue
- Formula: SUM(Total_Sales)
- Business Rationale: Primary indicator of 
  overall business health
- Target: 5-10% month-over-month growth

> KPI 2: Average Order Value (AOV)
- Formula: SUM(Total_Sales) / COUNT(Orders)
- Business Rationale: Rising AOV = successful 
  upselling; dropping = discounting/preference shift
- Target: Maintain above ₹1,30,000

> KPI 3: Revenue Concentration Rate
- Formula: High-tier Revenue / Total Revenue × 100
- Business Rationale: Reveals revenue dependency 
  risk for retention planning
- Target: Alert if above 75% or below 60%

> KPI 4: Electronics Revenue Share
- Formula: Electronics Revenue / Total Revenue × 100
- Business Rationale: Tracks single-category 
  dependency; healthy at 30-40%
- Target: Maintain 30-40% range

>  KPI 5: City Premium Index
- Formula: City AVG(Total_Sales) / Overall 
  AVG(Total_Sales) × 100
- Business Rationale: Identifies premium markets 
  for targeted launches vs volume markets
- Target: Above 110 = premium; below 90 = volume
